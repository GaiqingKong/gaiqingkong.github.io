I am currently a research fellow collaborating with Dr. Marine Vernet and Dr. Alessandro Farnè at INSERM U1028 & CNRL (Centre de Neuroscience de Lyon), France. Previously, I was awarded the Fyssen Foundation postdoctoral fellowship (2019-2021, https://www.fondationfyssen.fr/en/home/), hosted by Dr. Alessandro Farnè, Director of Research. I also worked with Prof. Patrick Haggard as a postdoc researcher at the Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience, UCL, UK (2018-2019), and with Prof Konrad Kording at Northwestern University and the University of Pennsylvania, US (Oct 2016- Jan 2018). I obtained my PhD degree from Peking University in 2018 supervised by Prof. Kunlin Wei, China. CV
