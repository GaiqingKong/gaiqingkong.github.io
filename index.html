I’m currently a professor in the Department of Psychology at Wuhan Sports University. Previously, I was a researcher collaborating with Dr. Marine Vernet and Dr. Alessandro Farnè at INSERM U1028 & CNRL (Lyon Neuroscience Research Centre), France. I was awarded the Fyssen Foundation postdoctoral fellowship (2019-2021, https://www.fondationfyssen.fr/en/home/ ), hosted by Dr. Alessandro Farnè, Research Director. I also worked as a postdoc researcher under the supervision of Prof. Patrick Haggard at the Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience (ICN), UCL, UK (2018-2019), and worked with Prof Konrad Kording at Northwestern University and the University of Pennsylvania, US (Oct 2016- Jan 2018). I obtained my PhD degree from Peking University in 2018 supervised by Prof. Kunlin Wei, China. CV
